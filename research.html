My academic work sits between statistical modelling and practical performance analysis. The central aim is to represent sporting decisions quantitatively without losing the tactical context that gives the numbers meaning.

Current Study

MSc by Research // Gaelic Football Analytics

The current research develops possession-value and shot models for Gaelic football using manually coded event data. The emphasis is on expected threat, transition structure and interpretable decision support.

Research Logic

What makes the work useful

Construct the data

Where commercial event feeds do not capture the required states, the research builds its own event dataset from video.

Model the process

Possession is treated as a sequence of transitions rather than a collection of isolated actions.

Return to the game

Model outputs are translated back into questions about progression, shot selection and team structure.
