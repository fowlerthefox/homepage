Model 01

Expected Threat

A transition matrix captures the probability of moving between possession states or exiting through a shot/turnover. Markov chain methods estimate the expected scoring value of each zone. The value of an action can then be expressed as the change between the origin and destination states.

Research Question

The two-point decision

Risk vs reward

When is the extra point worth the lower probability?

The new two-point arc creates a natural decision problem. A longer shot may return twice the score of a conventional point, but only if the reduction in conversion probability is not too large.

The research can frame this quantitatively by comparing expected return across shot locations, game contexts and shot types.

decision_rule.txt

> EXPECTED_RETURN_1 = P(score_1) × 1

> EXPECTED_RETURN_2 = P(score_2) × 2

> CHOICE = context + pressure + location + game state

> GOAL = quantify the trade-off, not prescribe every shot
